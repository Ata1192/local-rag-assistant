# Remote Work and Flexible Hours Policy

This document outlines the official guidelines, eligibility, and expectations for employees working remotely or utilizing flexible schedules at Acme Corp.

## 1. Eligibility Criteria
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day introductory period are eligible to apply for remote or hybrid work arrangements. 

* **Core Hours:** All employees must be available during our core collaborative hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST.
* **Role Dependency:** Roles requiring physical presence (e.g., Office Operations, Hardware Lab Techs) are not eligible for full remote status.
* **Performance:** Employees must maintain a "Meets Expectations" performance rating to qualify.

## 2. Equipment and Stipend
Acme Corp provides the necessary hardware to ensure a secure and productive remote working environment.

### 2.1 Hardware Provisioning
The IT department will ship a standard hardware bundle within 5 business days of approved remote status:
1. MacBook Pro 14-inch or ThinkPad T14
2. 27-inch Dell 4K Monitor
3. Logitech Web Cam and Jabra Headset

### 2.2 Home Office Stipend
Employees receive a one-time **$500 home office stipend** to purchase ergonomic furniture. This stipend is processed via regular payroll after submitting receipts through the Expensify portal.

## 3. Communication Guidelines
To maintain strong team alignment, remote employees must adhere to the following communication standards:

* **Slack:** Set status to active during working hours. Use "Away" or "In a Meeting" as needed.
* **Daily Standups:** Attendance is mandatory for your team's morning sync (typically 9:30 AM EST).
* **Video Etiquette:** Cameras should remain on during external client facing calls and internal team meetings.

## 4. Security and Compliance
Protecting company data is paramount. Employees must follow all protocols listed in the [Acme IT Security Guidelines](https://acme.corp).

> ⚠️ **Critical Rule:** Never connect to public Wi-Fi networks without activating the corporate NordLayer VPN.
